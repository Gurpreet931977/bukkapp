# BUKKAPP Business Console: Merchant Operations Guide

## 1. Non-Technical Philosophy

The BUKKAPP Business Console is designed specifically for **local business owners** (dentists, salon stylists, pickleball clubs, mechanics, AC technicians) with **zero SaaS or developer jargon**:

- Plain language: *"Manage your services"* instead of *"Manage service taxonomy"*, *"Set when customers can book you"* instead of *"Configure inventory availability"*, *"Customers"* instead of *"CRM"*.
- Built for mobile devices and laptops alike.

---

## 2. Core Navigation Structure

| Section | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | `/business/dashboard` | Answers: *What bookings do I have today? When am I available? How is my page performing?* |
| **Bookings** | `/business/bookings` | Filter by status (Upcoming, Completed, Cancelled, No-show). "+ Add booking" for direct phone calls. |
| **Calendar** | `/business/calendar` | Visual daily schedule. "+ Block time" for maintenance, breaks, or private events. |
| **Services** | `/business/services` | Service catalog with category templates (Dentistry, Sports, Barbershop, AC Repair, Detailing). |
| **Availability** | `/business/availability` | Regular weekly hours table with lunch break multi-periods and 1-click *"Copy Monday to weekdays"*. |
| **Customers** | `/business/customers` | Directory of past customers with visit counts, favorite services, and direct call actions. |
| **Reviews** | `/business/reviews` | Verified star ratings and inline official owner replies. |
| **My BUKKAPP Page** | `/business/profile` | Public storefront editor with *"Preview as customer"* live simulation. |
| **Settings** | `/business/settings` | In-app alerts, notifications, and storefront visibility pause controls. |
